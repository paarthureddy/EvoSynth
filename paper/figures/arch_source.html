EvoSynth — system architecture dynamic multi-armed-bandit meta-optimizer over GA, PSO, DE and CMA-ES
1 · DATA

Dataset registry

7 tabular datasets (Covertype, HIGGS, Adult, Airlines, Poker Hand, MiniBooNE, Jannis) + Fashion-MNIST for NAS
Metric per dataset: accuracy (multiclass), ROC AUC (binary)

Fixed split (data_seed)

Test rows: split off first, never searched
Search rows: cross-validation during search
Train rows: refit of the selected configuration

Latent search space [0,1]8

model_type: XGBoost · LightGBM · RandomForest · ExtraTrees
fe_strategy: none · stats · interactions
6 hyperparameters: learning_rate, n_estimators, max_depth, subsample, colsample_bytree, reg_alpha
Every optimizer works on the same vectors; decoding happens only at evaluation
2 · EVALUATION & INITIALIZATION

Fitness evaluator

k-fold CV on the search rows (k = 2)
Boosted models early-stop on a slice of the training fold
A failed fit scores 0 and is logged

Population generator

N random vectors (population size, default 40), evaluated in parallel threads

Stratified tier split

40% of N → Tier 1: 4 equal islands, each with a mix of good and weak members
60% of N → Tier 2 reserve: 2 equal groups, same mix
Split share and method (stratified, random, elite) are settings
3 · TIER 1 — COMPETITION
GAGenetic Algorithm · N/10 members
PSOParticle Swarm · N/10 members
DEDifferential Evolution · N/10 members
CMA-ESCovariance Matrix Adaptation · N/10 members
Each island runs t1_iters generations.
After promotion the two other families keep running in Tier 1 and can SWAP back in.
4 · SCORING & CONTROL

Composite score

min-max normalised over the islands scored together
Fitness0.30
Speed0.25
Efficiency0.15
Diversity0.15
Trend0.15
speed = gain since last check · efficiency = gain per eval-second · diversity = mean latent distance · trend = slope over 3 checks

UCB1 bandit

UCB = composite + c·√(ln N / nfamily)
c = 0.1 · ranks the families for every decision

Decisions

  • Promote the top 2 families to Tier 2
  • SWAP when the best Tier 1 UCB beats the worst Tier 2 UCB by > 0.10 (grace period with rollback, cooldown)
  • Inject fresh vectors into islands without improvement (PSO resets particles, CMA-ES restarts)
5 · TIER 2 — EXPLOITATION
Rank-1 family
its Tier 1 island + one reserve group
Rank-2 family
its Tier 1 island + the other reserve group

Pairwise elite sharing

The two islands exchange their top 20% every k iterations

Checkpoint every k iterations

Rescore all islands → SWAP / inject
k is sized automatically so Tier 2 reaches ≥ 2 checkpoints within the budget
6 · SELECTION & OUTPUT

Confirm before select

Top 5 distinct configurations re-scored with 5-fold CV; the best confirmed one is selected

Refit

Selected configuration retrained on the train rows (boosted models early-stop on a train slice)

Held-out test

Scored once on the test rows with the dataset’s metric; inference latency and model size measured
